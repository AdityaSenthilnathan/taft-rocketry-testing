TAFT ROCKETRY

Who We Are

Welcome to the official website of Taft High Rocketry! We are a student-led rocketry team based at Taft High School in Los Angeles, California. Our mission is to design, build, and launch high-power rockets while fostering a passion for STEM and aerospace engineering among our members.

Cone

Every flight begins at the nose. The cone guides airflow around the rocket, helping reduce drag while protecting the hardware inside. Its shape is a small detail with a big impact on how smoothly the rocket moves through the sky.

Flight Module

The flight module carries the electronics that help the team follow a flight and record what happens along the way. Careful integration keeps these components organized and secure, while the data they collect helps us learn from every launch.

Fuel System

The propulsion system provides the thrust that carries a rocket skyward. Choosing components that suit the vehicle and preparing the system with care are essential parts of building a reliable rocket and getting ready for launch.

Nozzle

At the base of the motor, the nozzle directs exhaust to produce thrust and send the rocket upward. It works together with the motor and airframe, making it a vital component despite its size.
